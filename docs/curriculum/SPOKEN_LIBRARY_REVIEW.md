# Spoken Library Review

## Scope

The current spoken library contains 53 episode scripts:

- 32 Intermediate episodes in the five day-grouped source files
- 10 Beginner lesson scripts
- 11 Advanced lesson scripts

## Review rules

The plain-English content check rejects unnecessary high-level general-English words such as:
- facilitate
- utilize
- commence
- consequently
- approximately
- sufficient
- comprehensive
- therefore
- intricate
- unprecedented

Technical vocabulary remains allowed when it is the exact technical term.

The conversation review reports a warning when an episode has:
- at least 97% strict A/B speaker alternation
- at least 45% of spoken turns containing three words or fewer

These are review signals, not hard measures of good writing.

## Current review result

The current source audit found:

- 0 banned-word hits across the reviewed library
- 0 episodes above the strict-alternation warning threshold
- 0 episodes above the short-turn warning threshold

The Advanced and Beginner scripts required a second dialogue pass because earlier drafts were too mechanically alternating. Intermediate already stayed below the warning thresholds.

## Status interpretation

A script being marked `script-ready` means:
- the text is complete
- the spoken English passed the language contract
- the dialogue passed the current structural conversation review

It does not mean:
- voice audio exists
- timing is aligned
- the episode is psychometrically validated
- the lesson has passed a human listening review

Audio synchronization remains independently gated by the timestamp manifest contract.
